About me

What drives me

What started as a fascination after watching the South Indian movie “Robot” when I was six years old, it’s hard to believe that what once felt like fiction is rapidly turning into reality.

Learning how to not only implement AI, but also effectively manage it, is crucial to building the right guardrails around its risks while actually reaping its benefits. That’s why I’m pursuing my Master’s in AI Systems Management at Carnegie Mellon, where I’m going beyond the buzzwords to develop a deeper understanding of how to build, deploy, and manage this transformative technology and the systems around it. It’s a full-circle moment to now study AI at one of its founding places.

I enjoy thinking critically and holistically about a problem, from the underlying system and technology to the product or solution that is ultimately delivered. It all comes down to creating and capturing value where it matters most.

My passion is to build for the better. And for that, my heart is always in the work.
